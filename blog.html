🔥 LiloanUnited is Now PrimeTactics!

After months of planning, growth, and reflection — LiloanUnited CFPH has officially rebranded to PrimeTactics. This isn't just a name change. It's a declaration of our evolution as a team, a community, and a competitive force in CrossFire Philippines. The same players you know and trust, now operating under a sharper, more elite identity. PrimeTactics represents our commitment to strategic excellence, disciplined training, and a community that pushes each other to be better every day. Welcome to the new era. Welcome to PrimeTactics.
