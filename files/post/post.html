iDate Full Screen Files

Step-by-step guide to install and play in full screen mode.

Release RAR Setting.rar
1 Locate Myth Games Launcher / Open File Location
2 Click Resources
3 Click Common
4 Click iDate ReVibe
5 Download the file
⬇ Download Setting.rar
  1. Step 6 — Download location: C:\Users\Administrator\Downloads
  2. Step 7 — Cut / Copy Setting.rar
  3. Step 8 — Go back to the iDate ReVibe folder
  4. Step 9 — Paste Setting.rar, right-click → Extract Here
Step 10 — That’s all. You can play now.
PS: Bawal po mag ALT+TAB kasi mag di-disconnect siya.
