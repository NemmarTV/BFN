Privacy Policy

How Prime Blog collects, uses, and protects information when you use our website and community tools.

Effective Last updated: August 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Prime Blog (“we”, “us”, “our”) handles information in connection with this website and related community features. We aim to keep things simple and transparent.

1. Who We Are

Prime Blog is a community esports platform operated by [Code.Dev] Nems (Discord: nemmartvy). This site is primarily a static website hosted on GitHub Pages.

2. Information We May Collect

2.1 Information you provide

  • Contact / Support form — name, Discord username, topic, optional IGN (in-game name), and message content when you submit the form.
  • Tryout / PROH1T applications — details you enter on application forms (e.g. IGN, role, rank, availability), which may be forwarded to Discord for staff review.
  • Donations — if you donate via GCash or similar, payment details are processed by the payment provider. We may see limited confirmation info (e.g. amount, reference) depending on how you send it.

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • Basic technical data — when you visit the site, your browser may send standard data (IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited). GitHub Pages and similar hosts may log this for security and operations.
  • Local storage / cookies — the site may use local browser storage for theme preference (light/dark), music player state, or similar UI settings. These stay on your device.

2.3 What we do not intentionally collect

  • We do not require account registration on this website itself.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not run aggressive third-party advertising trackers on the core pages.

3. How We Use Information

  • To respond to support requests and tryout applications
  • To manage clan / community operations (e.g. roster, scrims)
  • To improve the website and fix issues
  • To protect against abuse, spam, or security threats
  • To process optional donations and acknowledge support

4. Where Information Goes

  • Discord — form submissions and applications may be sent to our Discord server or staff DMs so we can reply. Discord has its own Privacy Policy.
  • Hosting — the website is hosted via GitHub Pages. GitHub’s privacy practices apply to server logs and hosting infrastructure.
  • External download hosts — links to MediaFire or similar are governed by those services’ policies when you download files there.
  • Payment providers — GCash / InstaPay process donation payments under their own terms and privacy policies.

5. Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell personal data. We may share limited information only when:

  • Needed for staff to handle your request (e.g. CMs reviewing a tryout)
  • Required by law or to protect safety and rights
  • You have asked us to (e.g. coordinating with a partner)

6. Data Retention

  • Support messages and applications are kept only as long as needed for community operations (typically until the request is resolved or the season ends, unless longer retention is needed for safety or disputes).
  • You can ask us to delete form-related data we control by contacting the owner on Discord.
  • Browser-local settings (theme, player state) can be cleared by you at any time via browser settings.

7. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information (e.g. limiting staff access, using standard hosting). No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. Please do not send passwords, bank PINs, or highly sensitive personal data through the contact form.

8. Children’s Privacy

The site is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has submitted personal information, contact us and we will take reasonable steps to remove it. Competitive tryouts may have higher age requirements as stated on those pages.

9. Your Choices

  • You can use the site without submitting forms.
  • You can request access to or deletion of form data we hold about you by messaging nemmartvy on Discord.
  • You can clear local storage / cookies in your browser.
  • You can stop using the site at any time.

10. Third-Party Services

Links to Discord, YouTube, MediaFire, Facebook, GCash, and others are subject to those companies’ privacy policies. We encourage you to review them.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date will change when we do. Continued use of the site after updates means you accept the revised policy. Important changes may be noted on the News page when practical.

12. Contact Us

Privacy questions or data requests:

  • Support form: contact.html
  • Discord: nemmartvy
  • Owner: [Code.Dev] Nems

This policy is written for a community website and is not formal legal advice. It describes our current practices in plain language.
