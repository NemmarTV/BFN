Rules Panel

Official Clan Rules

Follow these to stay in good standing. Updated for the PROH1T suite upgrade.

1. Respect & fair play

  • Zero toxicity — no insults, harassment, or discrimination
  • No griefing, throwing, or intentionally feeding
  • Take feedback calmly during reviews and scrims
  • Respect CMs, admins, and teammates at all times

2. Discord & communication

  • Discord is required for clan wars and announcements
  • No Discord = no clan war participation
  • Respond to pings from CMs within a reasonable time
  • Use correct channels; keep spam out of announcements

3. Identity & name format

  • Use your approved clan code name (e.g. BlanchePHT / PRETTY.PHT)
  • NameCard: Rose or Ghost as instructed by staff
  • Do not impersonate staff or other members
  • Keep IGN consistent with what you applied with

4. Tryouts, rank & activity

  • Open recruit — tryout is required before full roster
  • Target rank: Second Lieutenant and above
  • Age 18+
  • Show up for scheduled scrims; notify CM if you can’t
  • Long inactivity without notice may open your slot

Enforcement

Warnings → temporary restrictions → removal from roster for repeated or severe violations. Appeals go to Mamiplays / BlairePH, then owner nemmartvy if needed.
